How to Play Dots & Boxes

1
Draw Lines: Players alternate clicking between any two adjacent dots to draw a horizontal or vertical line.
2
Claim Boxes: Completing the 4th side of a 1×1 square captures the box, awarding you +1 point in your color.
3
Bonus Turns (Combos): Capturing a box gives you an immediate extra turn! Chain multiple box captures together for combo streaks.
4
Tactical Double-Cross: In high-level play, intentionally handing 2 boxes in a chain to your opponent can force them to open an even larger corridor for you!
